About Essence of Freedom™

Essence of Freedom™ is a wellness platform helping people reconnect with their deepest selves and step into their power — through ancestral, African-centered breathwork, coaching, and energy clearing. We clear the stagnant energy blocking your light, so your vitality, power, and joy can finally move. This is your unshrinking.

Meet the Founder

Nnemoma Chukwumerije, LMSW

I'm the firstborn daughter of Nigerian immigrant parents, raised to achieve and stay easy to manage. I followed that script through degrees at Howard, Columbia, and a career in non-profit and tech — and was quietly burned out the whole way. Getting let go from my job in 2024 was the wake-up call to pivot into the fullest, freest expression of my life.

I'm now a medicine woman, liberating others through African-centered breathwork, coaching, and energy clearing. Not because I read about it — because I lived it.
