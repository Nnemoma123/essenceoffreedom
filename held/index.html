What HELD is

HELD is a 3 month, 1:1 breathwork container, led virtually and built on Ndidi Breathwork™, African-centered breathwork rooted in Nigerian wisdom that weaves breath, sound, and movement with live African instruments and music. Ndidi means steadfastness in Igbo: a steady rhythm you keep, with someone tending the room for you.

The rhythm and movement take you deeper than breath alone. They clear stuck stress, wake your body up, and build your nervous system's capacity to hold more, so growth feels like expansion instead of strain. And because HELD is spiritually supportive, it reconnects you to Source, your intuition, and the purpose behind your business.

How HELD works

HELD is 3 months of 1:1 sessions, all virtual, with an onboarding call before we begin. You choose the rhythm that fits your season: weekly or biweekly.

Each 60 minute session follows the same rhythm, so your body learns what to expect and drops in faster each time.

  1. 1

    Arrive

    Check in with your body and your week. Land in the room.

  2. 2

    Breathe and move

    Guided breathwork and movement matched to that week's theme.

  3. 3

    Reflect

    Journal with a prompt that helps you name what came up.

  4. 4

    Express

    Talk it out loud with me and let it move.

  5. 5

    Integrate

    A closing practice and one small thing to carry into your week.

All you need to do is show up, breathe and receive.
