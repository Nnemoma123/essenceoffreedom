What Held is

Held is a 3 month, 1:1 breathwork container, led virtually and built on Ndidi Breathwork™, African-centered breathwork rooted in Nigerian wisdom that weaves breath, sound, and movement with live African instruments and music. Ndidi means steadfastness in Igbo: a steady rhythm you keep, with someone holding space for you.

The rhythm takes you deeper than breath alone. It clears stuck stress, wakes your body up, and builds your nervous system's capacity to hold more, so growth feels like expansion instead of strain. It is also spiritually supportive, reconnecting you to Source, your intuition, and the purpose behind your business.
