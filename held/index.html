What Held is

Held is a 3 month, 1:1 breathwork container, led virtually and built on Ndidi Breathwork™, African-centered breathwork rooted in Nigerian wisdom that combines breath, sound, and movement with live African instruments and music. Ndidi means steadfastness in Igbo, which is what this container gives you: a steady rhythm you keep, with someone holding space for you.

The rhythm of the instruments and music takes you deeper than breath alone. It wakes your body up, clears stuck stress and energetic blocks, and brings you back to your own aliveness, so you can hear your intuition again and make decisions from a grounded place. Over the three months, it also builds your nervous system's capacity to hold more, so the growth you are creating feels like expansion instead of strain. We begin with an onboarding call, and from there every session is shaped around you and what you are ready to open up to.

Held is also a spiritually supportive container, and that is where the depth comes from. Calming your nervous system relieves stress, and the spiritual work reconnects you to Source, your intuition, and the purpose behind your business. Together they give you more than a calmer week: they give you a deeper sense of being guided, which is what lets you expand with ease and trust your next moves.

What is in every session

Every Held session brings together four elements.

Breath

Guided breathwork clears stuck stress and energetic blocks held in your body, and reignites your vitality, so your nervous system can move out of high output and into aliveness, with more capacity to hold what you are building.

Sound, live African instruments, and music

The rhythmic sounds of the ekpiri, shekere, and drums, woven together with music, give your nervous system a steady rhythm to follow. Rhythm quiets a busy mind and brings your breath along with it, so you can drop out of leading and into receiving. With practice, your nervous system learns to settle faster and hold more.

Movement

Moving to the rhythm and music loosens the tension you carry in your shoulders and chest, and gives you a way to forget yourself and just be in the present moment. There is no role to play and nothing to get right.

Light language

I bring through light language for you, a multidimensional stream of sounds, tones and hand gestures, designed to communicate directly with your soul rather than your analytical mind.

How Held works

Held is 3 months of 1:1 sessions, all virtual, with an onboarding call before we begin. You choose the rhythm that fits your season:

  • Every other week: 6 sessions over 3 months
  • Weekly: 12 sessions over 3 months, for seasons with more stress, pressure, or change

Each 60 minute session follows the same rhythm, so your body learns what to expect and can settle in faster each session.

  1. 1

    Arrive

    check in with your body and your week, and land in the room.

  2. 2

    Breathe and move

    guided breathwork and movement matched to that week's theme.

  3. 3

    Reflect

    journaling with a prompt that helps you name what came up.

  4. 4

    Express

    talk through your thoughts out loud with me and process what came up.

  5. 5

    Integrate

    a closing practice and one small thing to carry into your week.

All you need to do is show up, breathe and receive.
