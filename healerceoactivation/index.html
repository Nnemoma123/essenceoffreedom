Before you decide

Questions you might have

I don't have a big following. Is that okay?

Yes. This starts with people who already know you: past clients, friends, family, and neighbors. You do not need a large audience.

Will this be a lot of work?

This is a mindset shift to step into your CEO energy and treat this like the sacred business it is, so you will put in real work, but it's built to fit your life. Each week you set aside a freedom block, protected time to take that week's actions, so you're never overdoing it.

What if I don't have past clients to reach out to?

We will plan a free community session and build your invite list from the people around you.

What if no one says yes?

Most healers need around 20 invitations before bookings start to land, and a no is information, not failure. We check in every week and troubleshoot together.

Is this only for breathwork guides?

No. If you lead in-person or live 1:1 sessions in any healing modality, this works for you.

Do I need an LLC to start?

Not to begin working through the program. You'll have access to a Business Basics resource when you're ready to file.

Which tier is right for me?

If you're self-motivated and ready to act with guidance, Tier 1. Choose Tier 2 if self-doubt tends to live in your body: you'll breathe with me every other week to clear it as it comes up, and we'll check in at the midpoint to keep you on course.
