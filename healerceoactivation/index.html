Before you decide

Questions you might have

Is this a group program?

No. It is 1:1 from start to finish. We work directly on your business, your medicine, and your community.

I don't have a big following or past clients. Is that okay?

Yes. If you want regulars or to be hired, you start with people who already know you and businesses near you. If you want to be seen, you build your audience from your first post. You do not need a large audience to begin. If you want to be hired or seen, you don't need past clients. If you want regulars, we plan a free or low-cost community session and build your invite list from the people around you.

Will this be a lot of work if I still work a 9 to 5?

This is a mindset shift to step into your CEO energy and treat this like the sacred business it is, so you will put in real work, but it's built to fit your life. Each week you set aside a freedom block, protected time to take that week's actions, so you're never overdoing it. This program was made for healers who still have a day job or part-time work. Your freedom blocks fit around the life you have now. The goal is a roster that can one day replace that job.

What if I am not sure I am ready, or no one says yes?

This is one of the questions I hear most. You do not need to feel ready. You have led sessions, and people felt your medicine. When doubt shows up in your body, we breathe through it together in our sessions. A no, or silence, is information, not failure. We check in every week and troubleshoot together.

Can I switch paths partway through?

You choose one path before we begin, so you can finish it. Finishing is what builds momentum.

Do I need an LLC to start?

Not to begin working through the program. Your Business Basics resource walks you through LLCs, liability waivers, and insurance when you're ready.

Which tier is right for me?

If you're self-motivated and ready to act with guidance, Tier 1. Choose Tier 2 if self-doubt tends to live in your body: you'll breathe with me every other week to clear it as it comes up, and we'll check in at the midpoint to keep you on course.
