Hi, I'm Nnemoma.

A few years ago I was working in corporate, and if you knew me then, you might not have guessed how burnt out I was. On the outside I was doing fine, but inside I was beyond exhausted, and I had a quiet feeling that I wasn't meant to stay there.

So I started asking for divine guidance about what was next, and I'm so grateful I listened, because a layoff led me out of that world and into becoming a breathwork facilitator.

Once I learned to work with my breath, I quickly felt clearer, more grounded, and calmer in my own body, and before long I knew I was meant to share it with other people.

Leaving a known path wasn't easy, but it's one of the best decisions I've ever made, and I'm so glad you're here for what comes next.

Then I was called deeper. I didn't see myself as a Black woman, or my people, represented in this industry, so I began leading as an African-centered breathwork guide. In my sessions I now weave breath, sound, and movement with African instruments and music to help people release the stress they've been carrying and feel more alive, awake, and free in their bodies. This is the heart of Ndidi Breathwork™.

Today I lead 1:1 and group sessions for companies, schools, and retreats. I also guide other healers into stepping fully into their divine gifts.

Want a peek at what a session looks like? Follow @ndidi.breathwork on Instagram.
